The Process

From Confused Student to Career-Ready in 4 Steps

SkillSync connects the whole loop: choose a path, learn with structure, practice with projects, and keep your thinking sharp.

01

Tell Us What You're Studying

Input your major, semester, and the courses you've taken. SkillSync's curriculum engine cross-references your exact coursework against industry skill requirements.

02

Discover Your Best Career Matches

Our rule-based scoring engine compares your skills, interests, and completed coursework against 46+ tech career paths, then explains why each path fits.

03

Get Your AI-Generated Roadmap

Our LLM engine generates a realistic, week-by-week learning roadmap tailored to your specific skill gaps — not a generic guide, but one built for YOU.

04

Learn, Track & Get Hired

Follow curated courses, build ranked projects, schedule study sessions, visualize hard concepts, and train with IQ & Aptitude challenges as your dashboard tracks growth.
